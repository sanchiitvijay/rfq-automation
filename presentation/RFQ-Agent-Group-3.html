01 · The problem
2 to 4 days of copy and paste for every RFQ

to build one RFQ by hand today
~40 a month
RFQs, across 6 countries
23 columns
to fill for every single product
📧 How it works today
A sourcing manager gets an email with product IDs, looks up every product across several sources, and copies each detail into the RFQ Excel before sending it to factories for a price.
⚠️ Where it goes wrong
Copy-paste slips across 23 columns, mixed units (1000 vs 1000 ft vs 333.33 yds), blanks filled by guesswork, competitor part numbers that quietly disappear, and no record of where a value came from.
02 · Why it matters
What this means for Ferguson
⚡ Speed
2 to 4 days become about a minute per RFQ. Each worker runs 4 RFQs at once, so nothing waits in line, and factories quote sooner.
🎯 Accuracy
Every value carries its source and a clickable link. Nothing is guessed: unknowns stay blank and are listed with the reason.
📈 Scale
People only review the orange and grey cells instead of typing 23 columns, so the same team handles many more RFQs a month.
💡
Our one line pitch: open the RFQ email in Gmail, click once, check the flagged cells, send the RFQ to the factory.

04 · Results
What our flow produced
  • Emails 1 to 4: 24 of 28 products filled; the 4 left are competitor part numbers
  • Email 1 matches the reference RFQ cell by cell (6 of 6 products)
  • "Not found" rows stay on the sheet, blank, with every source tried and the reason
  • About 10 to 90 s per RFQ live (rate-limited), ~2 s offline · 121 automated tests
05 · Our agentic arm
Where the agent made it better
Without the agent
Product 9677531: the catalog values are copied as they are. "Non-Detectable" in the name, aluminium backing in the details, Purple tape for a water line: nobody notices.
With the agent
Three cells turn orange, each with its reason: the APWA colour rule flags Purple for a water line, and GPT flags the "Non-Detectable" name and the Material against the aluminium backing. No value is changed.
⚖️ Agent or plain code?
Plain code for what can be checked: units, ranges, colour codes, missing cells. It is fast and testable. GPT only for cross-field contradictions, with strict JSON. It may never write a value, and if it fails the code checks still run.
